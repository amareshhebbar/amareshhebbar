### About
 
I build AI systems that do real work: teams of AI agents, tools that run AI models, tools that check what changed inside a model, AI that runs on your phone, and models trained for one specific job. I take them all the way from idea to a working product.
 
* **Founding AI Engineer at [Impossible AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/impossible-ai/).** Building the AI that runs directly on the phone for an [AI fitness app](https://humanovaminds.com/impossible-ai), and only uses the cloud when it has to. 80% of sessions run fully offline, with answers starting in about 45ms.
* **Applied AI Engineer at [AnacodicAI Labs](https://anacodicai.org/get-started)** (a nonprofit started at Boston University). Volunteering on ClinicalSearch, a team of AI agents that helps plastic surgeons find and check medical research.
* **Open source author.** Published **[Modeldiffr](https://pypi.org/project/modeldiffr/)** on PyPI. It shows exactly what changed when someone trains, shrinks, merges or edits an AI model, and whether the change is real or just random noise. Also published **[TrueNorth](https://pypi.org/project/truenorth-framework/)** (build AI chat from a simple config file, 1,258 tests, works with 8 AI providers, about 90% cheaper), **[BitNarrow](https://pypi.org/project/bitnarrow/)** (stops small models refusing safe requests, no training needed), and **[GitGrounded](https://pypi.org/project/gitgrounded/)** (catches AI mistakes before users see them).
* **Fixed a bug in Unsloth.** Fixed a crash that stopped Unsloth training from starting on Transformers 5.5 and newer ([PR #897](https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth-zoo/pull/897)).
* **Trained 16 medical AI models.** Published a **[set of 16 medical models](https://huggingface.co/collections/AmareshHebbar/medical-ai-fine-tuned-model-suite)** (Qwen2.5), each trained for one job: medical codes, billing codes, clinical terms, reading clinical notes, Indian health scheme packages, and Hindi medical help. Every model uses the same 4 step training recipe on real medical data, and the [training data is public too](https://huggingface.co/collections/AmareshHebbar/axismapper-medical-ai-suite).
* **Made small models faster and smarter.** **[ShortThink](https://github.com/amareshhebbar/ShortThink)** thinks out loud 42% less and answers 1.8x faster. **[ToolSure](https://github.com/amareshhebbar/ToolSure)** uses tools correctly 94.2% of the time (up from 81.2%). **[Stretch32K](https://github.com/amareshhebbar/Stretch32K)** reads 8x more text at once, trained on a single GPU.
* **Mentored 10 engineering interns** across frontend, backend and mobile, while shipping two AI products at the same time.
* **Hackathons.** SANS FIND EVIL! (AI for hacked computer investigations) · Google Cloud Rapid Agent (GitLab Partner) · INDIA RUNS (Redrob AI × Hack2Skill, Data & AI) · BITSoM Vertex Builders Pitch Fest (Top 150 of 2,300, Software Automation AI Track).
* **I prove my results.** Public benchmarks (100% correct findings on SANS forensics tasks), public training data, model comparisons with real statistics, and [every training run tracked online](https://wandb.ai/amareshhebbar-/axiomapper).
* Based in Bengaluru, India · **Open to remote AI engineering roles** (IST, happy to overlap with US and EU hours).
